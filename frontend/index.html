A clearer view of the figures.

Extract revenue, EBITDA, profit and segment performance. Compare periods, calculate supported ratios, and identify missing figures with page references.

FinBERT reads financial commentary. A small classifier provides an educational health rating, with transparent ratio rules when data is limited.

From report to perspective.

1. Upload a text-based PDF, up to 20 MB and 200 pages.

2. FinLens finds financial tables and extracts supported figures. Missing or ambiguous values stay empty.

3. Review ratios, management sentiment and the educational health rating. Check extracted figures against the original report.
